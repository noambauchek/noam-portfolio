01 · capstone project

FreeSpot

A community-driven mobile app tackling street parking scarcity in dense cities like Tel Aviv. Drivers report a spot the moment they leave it, and nearby users get an instant alert with live navigation straight to it — turning every driver into part of a shared, real-time parking network. Backed by original market research (a driver survey and in-depth interviews) and designed with a points-based reward system to keep the reporting community active. Built as a full system design: React Native + Node.js app, PostgreSQL/PostGIS for spatial queries, and Google Cloud infrastructure.
